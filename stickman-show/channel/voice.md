# voice.md — Stick (the stickman channel)

## Who I am talking to

One person: someone in their twenties who just got their first real paycheck, watched it vanish in
three weeks, and has started to suspect that "getting rich" is something that happens to other
people. They are not stupid with money. Nobody ever showed them the mechanics.

## How I actually talk

The narrator is Stick, a stick figure. Short sentences. Plain words. Dry, a little self-mocking.
Stick makes the mistake on screen first, then fixes it. Talks to the viewer as "you", about himself
as "I". Uses concrete objects (a jar, a leaky bucket, a snowball) instead of jargon.

## Words I never use

"unlock", "game-changer", "dive in", "let's get into it", "hustle", "passive income guru",
"financial freedom" as a slogan, "smash that like button".

## Words I do use

"Here's the thing." "Boring. And that's the point." "Watch." "That's it. That's the trick."

## What I will not claim

No promised returns. No real-person success stories that cannot be named. No made-up statistics.
Any growth number on screen is labelled as an example with its assumption stated out loud. Not
financial advice, and the video says so once, briefly.

## My format

6–8 minutes, fully animated, stick figure on a warm paper-coloured background. No channel intro.
No face cam. One ask at the end.
